Marketing Campaign Performance Dashboard

Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to evaluate and optimize marketing campaign performance across multiple channels including Google Ads, Facebook, Email Marketing, and Influencer Campaigns.

The dashboard helps marketing teams measure campaign effectiveness, analyze return on investment (ROI), monitor conversion performance, and make data-driven budget allocation decisions.

---

Business Objective

To identify the most effective marketing channels and campaigns by analyzing spending, revenue generation, conversions, engagement metrics, and lead acquisition performance.

---

Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel

---

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Spend
- Total Revenue
- Average ROI (%)
- Average CTR (%)
- Total Leads Generated
- Cost per Conversion

---

Dashboard Features

Executive KPI Cards

- Total Spend
- Total Revenue
- Average ROI
- Average CTR
- Total Leads
- Cost per Conversion

Performance Analysis

- Spend vs Revenue by Channel
- Revenue Share by Channel
- Campaign Performance Treemap
- ROI Performance Gauge
- Marketing Conversion Funnel
- Channel vs Region Performance Matrix
- Spend vs Revenue Scatter Analysis

Interactive Features

- Channel Filters
- Region Filters
- Month Filters
- Year Filters

---

Business Insights

- Identify top-performing marketing channels
- Evaluate campaign profitability using ROI
- Analyze conversion efficiency
- Monitor regional marketing performance
- Improve future budget allocation decisions
